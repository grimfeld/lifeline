# Using Lifeline on a phone

Lifeline is the same app on a phone, laid out for one thumb: going places at the foot, a page's
controls just above it, capture always in the middle.

## The header

- **☰** (top left) — opens the drawer.
  - Inside a Module it opens on that Module's own navigation; **All places** goes back to the
    full Rail.
  - Elsewhere it opens on the Rail: Modules, Surfaces, Bases, Screens, Settings, and the sync
    status.
- **The Workspace's name** — tap to switch Workspace, make a new one, or open Settings.
- **?** — opens the Guide page for where you are.

## The Phone bar

Five slots at the foot of every page:

- **Calendar** — far left, always. It opens on the view you last used on this phone.
- **One slot of yours** — left of the middle.
- **Capture** (**+**) — the middle, always. See [Capture](capture.md).
- **Two slots of yours** — on the right.

Your three slots can point at any Module, Surface or Screen, or a page inside a Module.

### Configuring the bar

In [Settings](settings.md) › **Phone bar**:

- Tap a slot to choose what it opens.
- Type a short label for it — what the slot says on the bar.
- **×** empties a slot.
- Each Workspace has its own bar; a new one starts from its Modules.
- If what a slot points at is gone (a Screen deleted, a Module removed), the slot stays empty —
  shown as **Set** — rather than being refilled. Tap it to choose again.

## The Phone dock

The strip right above the bar, where a page's controls sit, under your thumb:

- The Module's own tabs, against the bar.
- Above them, one line of the page's controls: its view switch (as a drop-down), date paging,
  search, its main **add** button.
- Any other filters fold behind one **Filters** button, which opens them in a sheet.
- Titles, quick-add fields and a single item's own buttons (back, edit, delete) stay at the top.
- When you type in a body, the editor's key bar sits above the dock, not over it.

## Capture

Tap **+** in the middle of the bar for the Capture sheet: this Workspace's capture Commands,
your pins on top. Hold any entry to pin it. See [Capture](capture.md).

## Screens and Cards

- A Screen's Cards stack in one column.
- **Edit** gives each Card buttons to move it up or down, make it shorter or taller, configure or
  remove it. See [Screens and Cards](screens.md).

## Peek and selection

- A [Peek](peek.md) opens as a sheet from the bottom; while you write in it, it takes the whole
  screen.
- Select words in a body and the Selection menu's button appears in the dock, clear of the
  phone's own copy/paste menu.

## Tips

- Install Lifeline to the home screen to share pages and text into it from other apps.
- Documents open to read; tap the text to start writing, so the keyboard never pops up
  uninvited.
