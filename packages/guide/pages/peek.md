# Peek and selection

Two ways to work with another Document without leaving the page you are on — the **Peek** and the
**Selection menu** — and how things from outside Lifeline get in: **Handoff**.

## Peek

A small view of one Document, opened beside where you are: a panel on the right on the desk, a
sheet from the bottom on a phone. The page behind stays in view.

- **When it opens** — clicking a link, a backlink, a search result or a chip on a Surface opens a
  Peek, unless you are already in that Document's own Module.
- **Ctrl+Enter** on a link in the editor peeks it, wherever it lives.
- **What it shows** — the Document's type and Module, its fields and its main actions, drawn by
  the App that owns it; your own Fields beneath.
- **Edit** — change its fields right there.
- **Write** (✎, or **⌘E / Ctrl+E**) — write its body with the Vault's editor. A Peek always opens
  to read; writing widens it.
- **Open in …** — go to the Document's Home Module.
- **Write in the Vault** — open its body in the [Vault](vault.md).
- **Back** — following a link inside a Peek replaces what it shows; Back returns. A Peek never
  opens on top of another Peek.
- Going to another page closes it.

## The Selection menu

Select some words in any Document's body — a Note, a Quote, a Thought, an Essay, a Day — to
link them or make something from them.

- **Open it** — the small button that appears above the selection (on a phone, in the Phone
  dock), or **Ctrl+.** (⌘. on a Mac).
- **Type** to filter what it offers.
- **Link** — on top:
  - **Link to …** the Document the words already name;
  - **Link to…** — search every Linkable Document. If the words differ from its name, you may
    tick **Also add "…" as an alias**.
  - The words become a `[[link]]`; you stay where you are.
- **Then each Module** — what it can make from the words, the Module you are in first:
  - Notes: a Quote; Projects: a Task; Knowledge: a Word, a Sentence or a Fact; House: a Need for
    the Shopping list; Calendar: an Event.
  - Your own types homed there (Journal: Person, Place). Types homed nowhere are under **Other**.
- An App's choice opens its own form, filled in with the words; nothing is saved until you save
  it.
- A type of your own is made at once, titled by the words, linked from them and opened in a Peek
  beside you; **Undo** on the toast takes it back. Offered only for a short, one-line selection.
- Only Document bodies offer it, never a form's input boxes.

## Handoff from outside Lifeline

A **Handoff** brings a page, a passage or a link in from outside: a title, the text, where it came
from.

- **The phone's share sheet** — share a page or text to Lifeline (once Lifeline is installed on
  the home screen).
- **The Lifeline browser extension** (Chrome-family and Firefox) — **Send to Lifeline**:
  - the toolbar button, **Alt+Shift+L**, or right-click › **Send to Lifeline**;
  - right-click also lists every kind of thing Lifeline can make ("Task", "Quote"…), so you can
    choose the receiver in one click, or **Choose in Lifeline…**;
  - it sends the selected text (else just the page's title and address), and the author and date
    when the page gives them. It reads nothing else and keeps nothing.

### The Handoff page

Where a Handoff lands.

- If you are signed out, it asks you to sign in first (or to work locally).
- It asks what to make (unless the sender already chose), and — when you have more than one
  Workspace — which Workspace it lands in, starting from the last one used.
- **Open the …** opens that App's own form, filled in. Nothing is saved until you save it.
- Close the form without saving and it says **Nothing was saved**; you can pick again.
- From the extension, its little window closes by itself once saved.
