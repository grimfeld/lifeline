# Screens and Cards

A **Card** is a small block that shows something live: today's chores, a streak, a Base View, one
Document. You place Cards on **Screens** you make yourself, and on the **Overviews** some Modules
open on.

## Screens

A Screen is a page of Cards you arrange, across Modules — a morning overview, say. The Modules
remain the main way in; Screens are the side door.

- **Open them** — **Screens** on the Rail, or **Go to Screens** in the palette. Each Screen is also
  in the palette under Go to.
- **New screen** — give it a name; it opens empty, ready for **Add your first card**.
- **Edit** — then:
  - **Add card** to open the card picker;
  - drag a Card to move it, drag its corner to resize (desk);
  - **Configure card** to change its settings or give it its own title;
  - **Remove card** — the Card goes; what it showed is untouched.
  - **Done** when finished.
- **Screen tag filter** — while editing, set Tags at the top: every Card that can filter by Tag
  then shows only items carrying any of them.
- **Delete screen** — in the **⋯** menu.
- Screens belong to the Workspace they were made in. Deleting a Workspace moves its Screens to the
  default one.

## Module Overviews

Money, House, Culture and Progress open on an **Overview**: Cards arranged by the Module, which
you may rearrange. Culture calls its Overview "Now", Progress "Today".

- **Edit** works exactly as on a Screen: add, move, resize, configure, remove.
- The card picker lists the Module's own Cards first, then every other Card.
- Your arrangement is per Workspace. Until you change it, it follows the Module's own default.
- **Reset to default** (in the **⋯** menu, once you have changed it) brings the Module's
  arrangement back.
- You may empty an Overview completely; **Add card** or **Reset to default** fills it again.

## Card instances

- The same Card can sit many times on one page with different settings: a "Push-ups" tile beside
  a "Weight" tile.
- Some Cards need a choice first (which View, which Document, which Stat): they show
  **Configure…** until you make it.
- A Card that can filter by Tag gets a **Tags** setting of its own, which narrows further within
  the Screen's filter.
- A Card that breaks shows **This card couldn't load.** with **Try again**; the other Cards keep
  working, and changing its settings tries it again too. A page that breaks says **Something went
  wrong on this page**, with **Reload** and **Go home**; going anywhere else clears it.

## The card picker

- **Search cards…** by name, description or App.
- Cards come grouped by App. Each Module's page in this Guide lists its own Cards.

## Lifeline's own Cards

These work everywhere, on any type:

- **Today** — everything scheduled or due today, across all Modules: today's Time blocks with
  their hour, Events, and work to tick.
- **Today's agenda** — today's timed items on an hour scale, with a line at now. Settings: from
  hour, to hour.
- **Map** — every located Document on a map; optionally one type only. See [Map](map.md).
- **Tags** — every tag in use, with counts; expand one to see what carries it.
- **View** — a saved Base View, in its own layout or another (list, table, cards, gallery, map,
  board, calendar). See [Bases](bases.md).
- **Document** — one chosen Document, its fields and body, live.
- **Graph** — one Document's neighbourhood: its links and backlinks, one or two steps out. See
  [Graph](graph.md).
- **Count** — how many Documents a View holds, as one big number.
- **Report** — a Report on a type or one Document: who comes up on the most Days, when it was
  last mentioned, a year as a heatmap. Over this month, this year or all time.

## Lifeline's own Commands

In the palette (Ctrl+K):

- **Go to Screens** — the Screens list.
- **Go to Vault** — the [Vault](vault.md).
- **Go to Bases** — the [Bases](bases.md) list.
- **Go to Calendar** — the [Calendar](calendar.md).
- **Go to Today** — Progress's Today page (see [Progress](progress.md)).
- **Go to Graph** — the [Graph](graph.md).
- **Go to Map** — the [Map](map.md).
- **Open … Base** — one per type ("Open Tasks Base", "Open People Base").

## On a phone

- A Screen's Cards stack in one column, in the grid's order.
- **Edit** gives each Card a strip: move up, move down, shorter, taller, configure, remove.
- A Screen can be put in a Phone bar slot. See [Using Lifeline on a phone](phone.md).
