# Journal

Living day to day in writing: one page per Day, a review for each week, month, quarter and
year, and the people, places and other kinds of page you write about.

Its sidebar (on a phone, the tabs in the Phone dock): **Today**, **This week**, **This month**,
**This quarter**, **This year**, **Types** and **Templates**.

## Journal

What you keep here: Days, Weeks, Months, Quarters and Years, your own Types (People, Places…)
and the Templates every kind of page starts from.

### Today — the Day page

- One page per day, titled by its date. Today's Day is made for you the first time you open it,
  from the Day's Template, so you can start typing at once.
- Page through days with **‹ ›**, jump back with **Today**, or pick any date from the calendar
  button. On a phone these sit in the Phone dock.
- Going back years: type a date or a period alone in the palette (Ctrl+K) — `2023-03-14`,
  `2023-W11`, `2023-03`, `2023-Q1`, `2023` — and pick the row at the top to open it.
- An older day with no page shows **Start writing**.
- On a wide screen a column sits beside the page:
  - the page's **links** — names in your text you could link, and what links to this Day;
  - the **month** as a small calendar, days you wrote dotted — tap one to open it;
  - **Reviews** — the week, month, quarter and year this day belongs to;
  - **On this day** — the same date in earlier years, with their opening words.
- On a narrower screen the links open from the **Links** button, its badge counting names
  still to link.

### The Daylog

- **What happened on a day, across Lifeline**: tasks completed, chores done, Events, entries
  logged, films and books started or finished, workouts, spends, fines, notes and quotes taken,
  practice in Knowledge…
- It sits at the top of every Day (the Day's Template starts with it) and is always live:
  it is read fresh each time, never written into your text.
- Each line links to the thing it came from.
- Place it on a Screen too, as the **Daylog** Card below.

### Reviews — week, month, quarter, year

- **Open this week's review** — opens the current Week. Where: the sidebar, or the palette
  (Ctrl+K).
- **Open this month's review** — opens the current Month.
- **Open this quarter's review** and **Open this year's review** — the current Quarter and Year.
  Where: the sidebar, or the palette.
- A review is a page of its own, started from its Template ("How the week went", "What
  mattered", "What I learned", "Next week").
- The current period's page is made when you open it; an older one shows **Start the review**.
- Below it, what lies inside: a Week's or Month's **Days**, each with its opening words and what
  happened on it ("not written" when there is no page); a Quarter's Months; a Year's Quarters.
- **‹ ›** pages through periods, **This week** (or month…) jumps back, and **↑** climbs to the
  longer stretch around it.
- The side column (or the Links sheet) adds who you mentioned most over the period, for each
  type that tracks it, with **More →** to that type's own page.

### Types

- Your own kinds of page — People, Places, whatever you need — with the fields you give them.
  A type made here lives in the Journal.
- **New type** — name it, give it fields and an icon.
- Each type lists its pages, with **New …** to add one, a pencil to edit the type, and a link to
  its Base for bulk editing.
- Open a type to see all its pages and its Reports — for example who was mentioned most — over
  this month, this year, all time or two dates you choose.

### Templates

- Every kind of page has its own Templates: the starting text for a new Day, Week, Note,
  Chapter, Person…
- Type a name and press **Template** to add one. The first one, named after the type, is the
  default; pick another when you make a page.
- A built-in kind with no Template of yours uses its own default.

### Commands

- **Open today** — today's Day. Where: the palette (Ctrl+K), or the sidebar's **Today**.
- **Go to date…** — keeps the palette open for a date or a period to type (`2023-03-14`,
  `2023-Q1`…); the row at the top opens it. Future dates open too, and nothing is made.
- **New note…** — pick a kind of page (Person, Place, Essay…), give it a title and, if you like,
  a Template; it opens straight away. Where: the palette.
- **New …** — one command per kind of page, such as **New person…**, for when you already know
  the kind. Where: the palette.
- **Quick capture** — one line appended to today's Day with the time. Where: the Capture sheet,
  the palette. Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter) saves.
- **Document types & templates…** — opens the Bases, where every type and its fields live.
  Where: the palette.
- **Clean up unused attachments…** — lists images no page embeds any more and removes them on
  request. Where: the palette.

### The Private folder

A folder whose pages are hidden until you unlock it with a password. Where: the palette.

- **Set up private notes…** — choose the password; shown until the folder exists.
- **Unlock private notes…** — shows its pages on this device.
- **Lock private notes** — hides them again.
- **Change private notes password…**
- **Turn off private notes…** — offered while it is unlocked.

### Cards

Place these on a Screen or any Overview.

- **Journal** — a day's page at a glance with its mood; click to write. Setting: day offset
  (0 today, -1 yesterday…).
- **Daylog** — what happened on a day, across every App. Setting: day offset; can be narrowed by
  Tags.
- **Quick capture** — one line into today's Day, Enter to send. Setting: timestamp or bullet.
- **Recent notes** — the pages you touched last, of one type or all.
- **People I've seen** — who your recent Days mention most. Settings: how many days back, how
  many, which type.
- **Mentions of…** — every page that links to one page you choose, newest first.
- **Unresolved links** — `[[links]]` that point at nothing yet; create the page in one click.
  Sort by most mentioned or by name.
- **On this day** — Days from the same date in earlier years; optionally also 1 and 4 weeks ago.
- **Journal streak** — the days you actually wrote: current and longest run.
- **Writing stats** — words this week and month, entries per month.
- **Pinned note** — one page, always in view, rendered or as plain text.
- **Folder** — the pages of one folder, newest first, subfolders optional.
- **Tag** — every page carrying one tag, with the text around it.
- **Random note** — a page at random, of a type or from a folder; reroll for another.
- **Saved search** — a full-text search over titles, aliases and text, kept on screen.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers **Quick capture** from the Journal: a line
for today's Day, stamped with the time.

## Ask Claude

The Journal has no tools of its own; Claude works with its pages as Documents.

- "What did I write last Tuesday?" — `search_documents`, `get_document`.
- "Add a paragraph to today's journal" — `update_document` (it replaces the whole text, so
  Claude reads it first).
- "Make a page for my friend Ana" — `create_document`, for your own types such as Person.

## Tips

- Select words in a Day to link them, or to make a Person or Place from them in one go.
- A quote can go straight into today's Day: **Insert into today's note** on any quote in Notes.
- The Day page stays light on a phone: links, Reviews and On this day are a tap away rather
  than on screen.
