# Money

Your money in one place: what you believe is left this month, what the bank says happened, and
what things cost at the shops.

Money opens on its **Overview**: what Pocket says is left, this month's Budget, your balances
and the upcoming bills, side by side. Move, resize, add or remove Cards as you like. Beside it:
**Pocket**, **Transactions**, **Budget**, **Reports** and **Prices**.

## Ledger

What you keep here: Accounts, Transactions and Categories — the facts from your bank. Balances,
spending and Budget figures are always worked out from them, never typed in.

On the **Transactions** page (four tabs):

- **Transactions** — every Transaction, newest first, with search and filters (Account,
  Category, month, kind, sort). Filters sit behind **Filters** on a phone.
  - **Uncategorized** — the filter for imported Transactions with no Category yet. A banner
    shows how many; tap it to see only those, or tap **Auto-categorize** to fill in Categories
    from your rules and history.
- **Accounts** — each Account with its balance. **New account**; **Reconcile** (type what the
  bank says; the difference is saved as an adjustment); **Import** (a CSV or OFX/QFX file
  exported from your bank — Lifeline never fetches it); the pencil edits or archives it.
- **Rules** — how payees get a Category when you import. Your own rules sit on top, and below
  them the guesses from your history. Pin a guess as a rule, or mark a payee **never** so it
  always waits for you (Amazon, say). A rule never rewrites your past by itself: **Apply to history**
  (the clock button on a rule with a Category) previews every Transaction it decides whose Category
  differs, past ones and the uncategorized inbox included. Untick any you want to keep, check the
  "Changes spending in N past months" note, then **Recategorize**. The result toast offers **Undo**, which puts each
  Transaction back in the Category it had (the inbox included) for as long as the toast stays up.
- **Recurring** — rent, salary, subscriptions, found by themselves after three regular payments
  to one payee. The next due day of each is listed; **Skip** waves one day off, and you can mute
  a series that isn't a bill.

Commands and Cards:

- **Add transaction** — an expense or income: amount, day, Account, Category, payee, note, tags.
  Where: the **+** button on Transactions, the Capture sheet, the palette (Ctrl+K), and a Card
  you can place.
- **Add transfer** — money moved between two of your Accounts (paying a credit card is one).
  Never counts as spending. Where: the palette.
- **Name PayPal payees** — hand over a PayPal activity file and Transactions the bank filed
  under "PayPal" take the real merchant's name. Where: the palette, or the banner on Transactions.
- **Go to Ledger** — opens Transactions. Where: the palette.
- **Go to Budget** — opens the Budget. Where: the palette.
- **Go to Categorize rules** — opens the Rules tab. Where: the palette.
- **Go to Money reports** — opens Reports. Where: the palette.
- **Balances** — Card: every Account's balance, with net worth on top.
- **Budget this month** — Card: what's available in each Category, worst first.
- **Upcoming bills** — Card: the next due days of your Recurring series.

### Budget

One month at a time; page with the arrows.

- Each expense Category shows spent, its Target and what's available. Red means over.
- Tap the Target to change it **for this month only**; clear it to go back to the usual Target.
- Tap a Category's name to edit it: name, group, monthly Target, and **accumulating** — a pot
  that carries over from month to month.
- **New category** adds one. A Category in use can't be deleted — merge it into another instead.
- Uncategorized spending is shown on its own: it counts nowhere until you categorize it.

### Reports

Your ledger over a stretch of months. Pick a range ("Last 12 months", "This year", "All time" or
your own two months) and filter by Account, Category, amount or text. Nothing here changes data.

- **Overview** — the period in five numbers, income against spending, balances, top payees.
- **Categories** — where the money went: a ring, a month-by-month stack and a sortable table.
- **Budget** — Targets against spending across the whole range, pots included.
- **Recurring** — fixed against variable spending, and what each series costs a year.

## Pocket

What you keep here: **Allowances** — sums you give yourself for a week, month, year or your own
span ("Eating out · €300") — and the **Spends** you take from them. A rough tally kept by hand,
separate from the Ledger: nothing here is ever a Transaction.

- **Spend** — take an amount from an Allowance, with an optional label; shows what's left after.
  **Save & add another** keeps the form open. Where: the **Spend** button on Pocket, tapping a
  jar, the Capture sheet, the palette.
- **New allowance** — name, amount, period, and an optional emoji and colour. Where: the
  **New allowance** button on Pocket, the palette.
- **Go to Pocket** — opens Pocket. Where: the palette.
- **Allowances** — Card: what's running today and what's left of each; tap one to spend from it.
  On the Money Overview it's titled "What's left".

On the Pocket page:

- **Now** shows running Allowances as jars, with the total left on top. Tap a jar to spend, the
  pencil to edit, the chip beneath to see its Spends.
- **Upcoming** and **Past** (folded) list the rest.
- When an Allowance ends, **Renew** (or **Renew all**) makes a fresh one for the next period —
  same name, amount and face, nothing carried over. Custom spans don't renew.

## Prices

What you keep here: **Products** you buy again, the **Shops** you buy them at (the chain, not
the branch), and **Receipts** — what you paid, line by line. From these, Prices shows the
cheapest shop and how prices change over time.

- **Add receipt** — a Shop, a day, the printed total and one line per item (label, price paid,
  amount, promo, Product). You can keep a photo of it beside the lines. If the lines don't add up
  to the printed total, it tells you. Where: the **Receipt** button on Prices, the Capture
  sheet, the palette.
- **Add price** — one price seen or paid: Product, Shop, price, amount. Leave **Bought it** off
  for a price you only noticed on a shelf or a flyer — it never counts as spending. Where: the
  **Price** button on Prices, the Capture sheet, the palette.
- **New product** — a name and what it's priced by: weight, volume, count or length, and the
  unit you read it in. A count can also be weighed (croissants: 4 pieces, 240 g) and compared by
  either. Where: the palette, and **+ Product** on the Products tab.
- **New shop** — a chain, e.g. "Lidl". Where: the palette, and **+ Shop** on the Shops tab.
- **Go to Prices** — opens Prices. Where: the palette.
- **Product price** — Card: pick a Product; shows its cheapest shop's usual price, every other
  shop, and the trend.

On the Prices page (five tabs):

- **Products** — each Product with its cheapest shop. Tap one for its own page: unit price per
  shop over time, statistics, and every price behind them.
- **Receipts** — every Receipt, newest first. Filter to bought or noticed only, or to
  **Unmatched** — lines not yet tied to a Product. Open a Receipt to match them.
- **Change** — how prices moved per shop over the last 3, 6 or 12 months.
- **Overview** — spending, shops, what you overpaid and what promos saved, over a span.
- **Shops** — your Shops.
- A banner appears when lines **need an amount** (a Product changed what it's priced by). Tap
  **Retype** to fill them in; until then they count as spent but not in unit prices.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers from Money:

- **Add transaction** (Ledger)
- **Spend** (Pocket)
- **Add receipt** and **Add price** (Prices)

A Ledger transaction and a Pocket spend stay separate on purpose: one is a bank fact, the other
what you believe you have left.

## Ask Claude

Through the Lifeline connector, Claude can:

- "Add a €12.50 expense for lunch at Café Rio" — `ledger_add`. It needs an Account and a Category
  unless the payee is one you've used before.
- "Spend €20 from Eating out" — `pocket_spend`; it tells you what's left.
- "Add this receipt" (from a photo you share) — `prices_receipt`. Claude reads the receipt;
  Lifeline doesn't. Missing Shops or Products are made first with `prices_shop_add` and
  `prices_product_add`.
- "Milk is €1.09 at Lidl" — `prices_add`; saved as noticed unless you say you bought it.
- Find or read anything here — `search_documents`, `get_document`.

## Tips

- Nothing goes in the Ledger before it happens: no planned payments. Upcoming bills are read off
  your history and disappear when the bank's import brings the real Transaction.
- Imported Transactions without a Category are an inbox, not an error — clear them when you have
  a minute; **Auto-categorize** does most of it.
- An Account in use can't be deleted; archive it to keep its history out of the way.
- Use an **accumulating** Category for things you save up for (holidays, car repairs). Pocket
  Allowances never roll over.
- On a phone, page controls and **Filters** sit at the bottom, above the Phone bar.
