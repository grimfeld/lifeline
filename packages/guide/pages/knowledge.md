# Knowledge

Keeping what you chose to keep from being forgotten: words, sentences, facts and quotes,
practised a little each day by recalling them. Knowledge never picks what you learn: each thing
here is one you added, in your own words.

## Knowledge

What you keep here: **Topics** (Spanish, History, Philosophy…), and in each one **Words**,
**Sentences** and **Facts**, plus any Quotes you keep from [Notes](notes.md). **Decks** narrow a
Topic to a set you choose.

- **Word** — a term and its meaning; practised both ways (term → meaning, meaning → term).
- **Sentence** — a sentence with blanks in double braces, `Tengo {{ganas}} de comer`, and an
  optional translation; each blank is practised on its own.
- **Fact** — a question and its answer, both in your words; practised one way.
- Each can note where you met it: a link to the page it came from, a URL or a few words.

### Topics

- One Topic is open at a time: switch it in the header. **New topic** (the **+**) makes one.
- The settings button beside it opens the Topic's settings:
  - **Name**;
  - **New per day** — at most this many new prompts enter practice each day, always after what
    is due (10 unless you change it);
  - **Tallies** — count a day you practised towards something you track elsewhere;
  - **Archive** — out of the switcher and out of practice, everything kept. Archived Topics are
    listed at the foot; tap one to bring it back. A Topic that holds nothing can be deleted
    instead.

### Practice

- How much is ready: due today, new today, and how many more new are waiting.
- **Practise** the open Topic, one of its **Decks**, **Everything** across all Topics, or what
  you kept **From a source** — a book, a podcast, with its episodes.
- In a practice: try to recall the answer, **Show answer**, then grade yourself **Pass** or
  **Fail**. Keys: Space reveals, 1 or F fails, 2 or J passes. **Undo** a mis-tap. **Stop** any
  time — nothing is lost.
- **Retention** — the share of recalls passed over the last 30 days, for this Topic and for all.
- **The week ahead** — how many prompts fall due each day.

### Kept

- Everything in the open Topic: search it, show only Words, Sentences, Facts or the paused ones,
  or one Deck. On a phone the choices sit behind **Filters**.
- Each shows when it is next due. **Pause** takes it out of practice and keeps its history;
  **Resume** puts it back.
- **+** adds a Word, a Sentence or a Fact.
- **Also kept here** — Quotes kept in this Topic from Notes.
- Open one to edit it, see its prompts, and put it in or out of Decks. Editing never resets its
  practice.

### Decks

- A Deck is a set you choose inside one Topic ("Kitchen", "Work emails"), to practise on its own.
- Name it and tick what it holds. Deleting a Deck keeps its words.

### Commands

- **Add word…** — a term and its meaning in a Topic. Where: the Capture sheet, the palette
  (Ctrl+K).
- **Add sentence…** — a sentence with its blanks. Where: the Capture sheet, the palette.
- **Keep a fact…** — a question and its answer. Where: the Capture sheet, the palette.
- **Practise everything** — starts a practice of all that is due in every Topic. Where: the
  palette.
- **Go to Knowledge** — Where: the palette.

### From another page

Select text in a Note, a Quote or any page — or send a web page with **Send to Lifeline** — and
choose **Word**, **Sentence** or **Fact**. The form opens with the selection and remembers where
it came from; for a Fact, the selection stays beside you while you write the question and answer.

### Cards

- **Due today** — everything due, by Topic; one tap into practising everything.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers, under Knowledge: **Add word…**,
**Add sentence…** and **Keep a fact…**.

## Ask Claude

Claude adds only what you dictate, word for word — it never words a meaning, a question or an
answer for you.

- "Add to Spanish: *tener ganas de* — to feel like" — `knowledge_word`.
- "Add the sentence *Tengo {{ganas}} de comer*, meaning *I feel like eating*" —
  `knowledge_sentence`.
- "Keep a fact in History: When was the Peace of Westphalia? 1648" — `knowledge_fact`.
- Adding the same thing twice is refused, not duplicated.
- Practising is yours: Claude does not grade recalls.

## Tips

- Keep **New per day** low after a big day of adding: what is due always comes first.
- Use **From a source** to go over everything you kept from one book before a discussion.
- Words and Facts are not link targets: write about the subject itself in [Notes](notes.md).
