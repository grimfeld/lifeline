# Manuscript

Creative work written without distraction: a novel, a collection of stories or poems, a play —
written chapter by chapter.

## Manuscript

What you keep here: Manuscripts, each made of ordered Chapters, with its outline and plan, and
the Outtakes you cut from it.

### Manuscripts — the shelf

- Every work, with its form (Novel, Stories, Poems, Play, Other), its status (Draft, Revised,
  Done), how many chapters and how many words.
- **New manuscript** — a working title and a form; it opens straight away.

### Writing a Manuscript

- The **binder** on the left (on a phone, behind **Binder** in the Phone dock):
  - **Outline & plan** — the Manuscript's own page, for its outline and notes to yourself;
  - **Chapters** in order — move one up or down with the arrows; **Chapter** adds a new one;
  - **Outtakes** — what you cut, kept.
- The page in the middle. **Focus** (the expand icon) takes everything else away; **Esc**
  leaves it.
- Each Chapter has its own status.

### Outtakes

- Select a passage in a Chapter and press the **scissors**: it moves to the Outtakes, so cutting
  never loses words.
- In **Outtakes**, choose a Chapter under **Restore into** and restore a passage to its end, or
  delete it for good.

### Versions

- A Chapter keeps Versions like an Essay: in the side panel, save the current text with an
  optional label, compare a version with the current text, and **Restore** it as a new edit.
  See [Research](research.md).

### Commands

- **Go to Manuscripts** — the shelf. Where: the palette (Ctrl+K).

### Cards

Manuscript has no Cards of its own.

## Capture

Manuscript has nothing on the Capture sheet: a work is written, not captured.

## Ask Claude

Manuscripts and Chapters are Documents, so Claude works with them through the general tools.

- "How long is chapter three now?" — `search_documents`, `get_document`.
- "Set *The Lighthouse* to Revised" — `update_document`.

## Tips

- Keep the plan in **Outline & plan**, not in a Chapter: it travels with the work, outside the
  text itself.
- Cut boldly: everything you cut waits in the Outtakes.
