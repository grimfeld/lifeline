# Culture

What you read, watch, listen to and play, and the art you love. Culture opens on **Now**, then has
a page for each shelf: Library, Cinema, Discotheque, Arcade and Museum.

## Now

Everything you have on the go, on one page: what you're reading, watching, listening to and
playing, plus today's artwork from the Museum.

- It is made of Cards. Move, resize, add or remove them; **Reset to default** brings back the
  original layout.
- Each Workspace keeps its own arrangement.

## How the shelves work

Library, Cinema, Discotheque and Arcade work the same way. A book, film, album or game is a
**work**.

- **Gallery**: the page you land on. A grid of covers for everything in that App.
- **Shelf**: where a work stands with you: *To read / watch / listen / play*, *In progress*,
  *Done* or *Dropped*. Pick a shelf above the Gallery. On a phone it is a select in the Phone dock.
- **Search** looks at the title, the creator, the reason you added it and tags. Start with `#` to
  search tags only.
- **Filters**: pick a medium (Books, Comics, Manga…) and a sort (title, recently added, recently
  finished, year). On a phone they open from the **Filters** button.
- **Quick add** at the top: type `Title — Creator` (or `Title by Creator`), pick the medium and
  press Enter. The work goes onto the *To…* shelf. If lookup finds matches, pick one to get its
  cover and details too. The toast lets you **Undo**. A work you already have is not added twice,
  and the toast offers to **Open** it.
- **Add with details…** opens the full form.
- Tap a cover to open the work's detail. There you can start it, finish it, drop it, edit it,
  find metadata, refresh it from its source, or delete it. In the Library, Cinema and
  Discotheque you also write your **Review** there. The **⋯** menu on the tile offers the same
  moves.
- **Marks** (Library, Cinema, Discotheque): a heart for **Liked**, and one to five stars. Neither
  moves the shelf.
- **Parts**: track the chapters, issues, episodes or tracks of a work (not films or games) in the
  detail.
  - Tap a number to mark it read (watched, heard), or switch to **Tap opens** to give a Part its
    own title, Marks and Review.
  - **Caught up to…** marks everything up to a number as done before Lifeline: no days, nothing
    in the Daylog.
  - The run's length can be left blank while it is still going.
  - The first Part you read starts the work. The last one *offers* to mark it done but never does
    it for you.
- Starting, finishing, buying and Parts read show up in that day's Daylog.

## Library

Books, comics and manga.

- **Nightstand** tab: the few things claiming your attention now, in progress or next up. It has
  5 slots for books, 3 for comics and 3 for manga. **Slot it** from a work's detail or ⋯ menu.
  Finishing or dropping a work frees its slot.
- Each tile has quick toggles: **Read**, **Bought** (Wanted ⇄ Owned) and the heart.
  **Gone (sold / returned / given away)** is in the ⋯ menu.
- **Authors** have their own page, reached from a byline: your works by them, and their
  **Bibliography** from Open Library, where you can tick works and add them to *To read* in one
  go. From an Author's page you can find them on Open Library, edit their name and aliases,
  **Merge…** two Authors who are one person, or open them in the Vault.

Commands (Ctrl+K):

- **Add book…**: one line (title, author, kind) onto *To read*. Also in the Capture sheet.
- **Read next part…**: pick a book, comic or manga; its next chapter or issue is read today. Also
  in the Capture sheet.
- **Add book with details**: the full form.
- **Go to Library**: opens the Library.
- **Resolve authors…**: matches your Authors to Open Library. A single exact-name match is applied
  for you; you pick the rest.
- **Library lookup settings…**: your ComicVine key (comics) and an optional Google Books key.

Cards you can place:

- **Nightstand**: what holds a slot, with **+1** for the next Part. It can show one medium.
- **Book wanted list**: books you want but don't own, Nightstand ones (buy now) first. Tick
  **Got it** to mark one owned.
- **Reading now**: what you're reading, with **+1** for works whose Parts you track.
- **Finished reading**: how much you read in a year, with the latest covers.
- **Surprise me**: one random unread pick. Reroll it, or slot it.
- **Library quick add**: the one-line add, on a Card.
- **To read**: what's waiting on the shelf, with a one-tap start.

## Cinema

Movies and series, streamed rather than owned.

- **Playlists** tab: your own ordered lists ("Films for a rainy Sunday"), with an optional blurb
  and an option to show them ranked. Add a work from its ⋯ menu. A Gallery filter shows only the
  works you've Liked.

Commands (Ctrl+K):

- **Add movie…**: one line (title, director, movie or series) onto *To watch*. Also in the Capture
  sheet.
- **Mark watched…**: pick a film or series, mark it watched, then give it stars or a heart. Also
  in the Capture sheet.
- **Watch next episode…**: pick a series; its next episode is watched today. Also in the Capture
  sheet.
- **New playlist…**: starts a Playlist.
- **Add movie with details**: the full form.
- **Go to Cinema**: opens the Cinema.
- **Cinema lookup settings…**: your TMDB key, for posters and details.

Cards you can place:

- **Watching now**: what you're watching right now.
- **Watched this year**: how much you watched, with the latest posters.
- **Surprise me**: one random unwatched pick. Reroll it, or start it.
- **Cinema quick add**: the one-line add, for movies or series.
- **To watch**: what's waiting, with a one-tap start.

## Discotheque

Albums, streamed rather than owned. Lookup comes from MusicBrainz and needs no key.

- **Playlists** tab: works as in the Cinema, for albums.

Commands (Ctrl+K):

- **Add album…**: one line (album, artist) onto *To listen*. Also in the Capture sheet.
- **Mark listened…**: pick an album, mark it listened, then give it stars or a heart. Also in the
  Capture sheet.
- **Hear next track…**: pick an album; its next track is heard today. Also in the Capture sheet.
- **New playlist…**: starts a Playlist.
- **Add album with details**: the full form.
- **Go to Discotheque**: opens the Discotheque.
- **Enrich albums from MusicBrainz…**: fills in covers and details for albums that have none.
  Exact title matches are applied for you; you pick the rest.

Cards you can place:

- **Listening now**: what you're listening to right now.
- **Listened this year**: how much you listened, with the latest covers.
- **Surprise me**: one random unheard pick. Reroll it, or start it.
- **Discotheque quick add**: the one-line add, on a Card.
- **To listen**: what's waiting, with a one-tap start.

## Arcade

Games: bought, played, finished. There is no Nightstand, no Parts and no Marks.

- A game can be Wanted or Owned. Use **Got it (owned)** or **Gone** in its ⋯ menu.

Commands (Ctrl+K):

- **Add game…**: one line (title, developer) onto *To play*. Also in the Capture sheet.
- **Add game with details**: the full form.
- **Go to Arcade**: opens the Arcade.
- **Arcade lookup settings…**: your RAWG key, for covers and details.

Cards you can place:

- **Game wanted list**: games you want but don't own, oldest wish first.
- **Playing now**: what you're playing right now.
- **Played this year**: how much you played, with the latest covers.
- **Surprise me**: one random unplayed pick. Reroll it, or start it.
- **Arcade quick add**: the one-line add, on a Card.
- **To play**: what's waiting on the shelf, with a one-tap start.

## Museum

Classical art you care about, and exhibitions you curate from it. The art forms are paintings,
drawings, photographs, sculptures and architecture.

- **Now hanging**: posters of the exhibitions on the walls. Tap one to walk through it, room by
  room.
- **Holdings** tab: every artwork as an image grid. Filter by art form, search by title or artist,
  and tap one to edit it. An eye means you've seen it in person.
- **Exhibitions** tab: every show and its state: *In preparation*, *Hanging* or *Archived*. Use
  **Hang**, **Take down** (archives it, rooms intact) or **Reopen** (back to preparation).
  Delete a show that isn't hanging.
- An exhibition in preparation is where you curate. Add rooms, hang artworks in a room with
  **Hang…**, order them, and order the rooms.
- **Add artwork** takes the art form, title, artist, year, location, materials and dimensions, an
  image URL, *Why it's in my museum* and tags. Search Wikidata from the title. A building can
  have coordinates.

Commands (Ctrl+K):

- **Add artwork…**: adds an artwork to the Holdings. Also in the Capture sheet.
- **Go to Museum**: opens the Museum.
- **Go to Exhibitions**: opens the Museum, where the Exhibitions tab is.

Cards you can place:

- **On display**: one artwork from your hanging exhibitions, different every day. Set it to one
  exhibition, or leave it on any.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers Culture's adds under three broader entries.
Each one asks which kind of thing you mean, then opens that App's own form:

- **Add to Culture…**: Book, comic or manga · Film or series · Album · Game · Artwork.
- **Mark done…**: Watched a film or series · Listened to an album.
- **Next part of…**: Next chapter or issue · Next episode · Next track. Type a few letters and tap
  the work; things in progress are listed first.

Each one you fold into these is still in the palette, and you can pin it to the top of the Capture
sheet on its own.

## Ask Claude

Through the Lifeline connector, Claude can:

- Add a work: "Add Dune by Frank Herbert to my reading list" (`library_quick_add`),
  `cinema_quick_add`, `discotheque_quick_add`, `arcade_quick_add`.
- Mark something watched or listened, with stars or a like: `cinema_mark_watched`,
  `discotheque_mark_listened`.
- Read the next Part: "Next episode of Severance" (`cinema_next_part`), `library_next_part`,
  `discotheque_next_part`. It tells you when that was the last one, so you can mark the work done.
- Add an artwork to the Museum: `museum_add`.
- Find or read any work with `search_documents` and `get_document`.

Claude never adds a twin. If the work is already on your shelf (or the artwork is already in the
Holdings), it tells you and changes nothing.

## Tips

- Track Parts in the work's detail first. **+1** and "Next part of…" offer only works whose Parts
  you track, or that are in progress.
- **Done** is one click from any shelf, and clicking again undoes it. Undoing never takes back a
  freed Nightstand slot, so re-slot it yourself.
- A work you've read but still want to buy stays on the Wanted list. The shelf never moves it.
- Lookup never holds you up: Enter adds what you typed, even while matches are still loading.
