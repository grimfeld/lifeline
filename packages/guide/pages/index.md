# Getting around

Lifeline keeps your life in one place: tasks, chores, money, books, notes, days. This page shows
how it is laid out and lists every page of the Guide.

## What Lifeline is made of

- **Documents** — everything you keep is a Document: a Task, an Event, a Chore, a Note, a Day, a
  film. Each has one **type**, which decides its fields. See [Documents](documents.md).
- **Modules** — what you open: House, Projects, Money, Journal… Each is written for its own
  domain, with its own layout and navigation.
- **Surfaces** — places that show Documents of many types together: the [Calendar](calendar.md),
  the [Map](map.md) and the [Graph](graph.md). They belong to no Module.
- **Bases** — one table per type, for sorting, filtering and bulk edits. See [Bases](bases.md).
- **Screens** — pages of Cards you arrange yourself. See [Screens and Cards](screens.md).
- **Workspaces** — separate worlds of data, each with the Modules it needs. See
  [Workspaces](workspaces.md).

## The Rail (on the desk)

The narrow column of icons at the far left. It is how you go *between* places.

- **Top** — the Workspace picker (its first letter). Click it to switch, or to make a new one.
- **Then** — this Workspace's Modules.
- **Then** — the Surfaces: Calendar, Map, Graph.
- **Then** — Bases and Screens.
- **Foot** — Settings, the sync status, and the Guide button, which opens the Guide page for
  wherever you are.

Everything to the right of the Rail belongs to the Module you opened.

## On a phone

- **Header** — `☰` on the left, then the Workspace's name (tap it to switch).
- **☰ drawer** — inside a Module it opens on that Module's own navigation; tap **All places** for
  the full Rail. Elsewhere it opens on the Rail.
- **?** in the header — opens the Guide page for where you are.
- **Phone bar** — five slots at the foot: Calendar, one slot of yours, **Capture**, two more of
  yours.
- **Phone dock** — just above the bar: the page's own controls, under your thumb.

More in [Using Lifeline on a phone](phone.md).

## Tips

- Lifeline reopens where you last were, per Workspace and per device.
- **Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac) opens the command palette from anywhere. See [Capture](capture.md).
