# Notes

What you take from what you read, watch and listen to: each **Source** gathers your Notes on it
and the Quotes you kept from it. Beside them, **Windows** show things from outside Lifeline.

Its sidebar (on a phone, the tabs in the Phone dock) lists **Sources** and **Quotes** under
Notes, **Windows** under Windows, and the Bases of their types at the foot.

## Notes

What you keep here: Sources (a book, a film, an article, a podcast episode…), Notes — your own
words about a Source — and Quotes — passages taken from one, word for word.

### Sources — the shelf

- Every Source, shelved by kind: books, films and albums first, then articles, podcasts,
  episodes, videos, websites, papers, talks. The ones you took most from lead each shelf.
- Each shows how many notes and quotes you took from it.
- Search by title, author or `#tag`.
- **New source** — a name, a kind and an optional URL; the rest is filled in on its page.
  Books, films and albums are Sources already: add those in [Culture](culture.md).
- **Note or quote…** — take one from any Source without opening it first.

### A Source's page

- What it is: kind, author, link, and the Source it is part of (an episode's show, an article's
  magazine).
- A form right there to **take a note or a quote** from it, with where (page, chapter, 34:12).
- **Inside it** — its episodes or articles, when it has any. A show's page gathers what you took
  from its episodes too.
- Its **Notes**, newest first, and its **Quotes**.
- For a book or a film, **Open its own page** goes to it in Culture.

### Quotes

- Every quote, grouped by its Source.
- Search quotes, comments, titles and `#tags`. Narrow by kind of Source or one Source; sort by
  newest, oldest or random. On a phone these sit behind **Filters**.
- On each quote: **Edit**, **Insert into today's note** (adds it to today's Day in the
  [Journal](journal.md)), **Copy as blockquote**, **Delete**.
- **Keep** (the brain icon) — practise recalling it in [Knowledge](knowledge.md): pick a Topic
  the first time; after that it is kept, stopped and kept again in that Topic. Stopping keeps its
  past practice.

### Notes and Thoughts

- A Note always has a Source. Give a [Thought](thoughts.md) a Source and it becomes a Note on
  it; take a Note's Source away and it is a Thought again. Links to it survive either way.

### Commands

- **Note on a source…** — your words on a Source, with where in it. Where: the Capture sheet,
  the palette (Ctrl+K).
- **Quote from a source…** — a passage, word for word, with where and a comment. Where: the
  Capture sheet, the palette.
- **New source…** — opens the shelf to add one. Where: the palette.
- **Go to Notes** — the shelf of Sources. Where: the palette.
- **Go to Quotes** — every quote. Where: the palette.

The note and quote forms offer the Sources you are in now first, then every Source, and
**＋ New source…** to make one as you go.

### Keep as a Quote

Select text in any page, or send a web page with **Send to Lifeline**, and choose **Quote**:
trim the text, pick or make its Source, and keep it. Nothing is saved until you do.

### Cards

- **Random quote** — one quote from your Sources; reroll for another. Settings: a kind of Source,
  one tag.
- **Quote of the day** — the same quote all day, another one tomorrow. Same settings.
- **Quotes of…** — the quotes you kept from one Source.
- **Recent quotes** — the latest quotes, with their Sources.
- **Note or quote capture** — take a note or a quote from what you are in now, right on the
  Screen.

## Windows

What you keep here: things from outside Lifeline shown inside it, which Lifeline does not own.
Four kinds:

- **Embed** — a web page shown as-is in a frame. Pages that refuse to be framed stay blank: use
  **Open** for those. Can reload on a period.
- **Feed** — the latest items of an RSS or Atom feed, opened outside; new ones are marked.
- **Calendar subscription** — an iCal link whose events appear, read-only and in their colour,
  on the calendar, week and schedule. Nothing is copied, moved or completed.
- **Live value** — one number read from a URL every few minutes (a JSON path or a regex), shown
  with a prefix and unit. It shows when it was last read and goes stale rather than guess.

### The Windows page

- Every Window by kind. **New** adds one. Tap a Window to see it full size; its button opens the
  URL outside Lifeline, and its menu edits or deletes it.
- Each takes a name, a URL, Tags, and how often to refresh. Request headers are optional.

### Commands

- **New embed** — Where: the palette (Ctrl+K).
- **New feed** — Where: the palette.
- **New calendar subscription** — Where: the palette.
- **New live value** — Where: the palette.

### Cards

- **Embed** — a web page on a Screen: a dashboard, a doc, a video.
- **Feed** — a feed's latest items; new ones are marked, and seen once the Card has been on
  screen a moment.
- **Live value** — one number as a tile: weather, a price, a count.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers, under Notes: **Note on a source…** and
**Quote from a source…**.

## Ask Claude

- "Note on *Dune*: the desert as a character, page 112" — `commonplace_note_add`.
- "Keep this quote from the podcast: …" — `commonplace_quote_add`, word for word.
- "Add the article *The Tyranny of Structurelessness* as a source" — `commonplace_source_add`,
  with its kind, link, author and the Source it is part of.
- A Source is named by its exact title (or id), so Claude may ask which one you mean.

## Tips

- Start from the Source: its page is where its notes and quotes live and where new ones start.
- A show and its episodes: add the episode as **part of** the show, and the show's page gathers
  both.
- Windows have no Capture: set them up once from the palette or the Windows page.
