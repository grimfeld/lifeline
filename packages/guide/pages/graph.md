# Graph

The **Graph** draws everything you can link to as a dot, and every link between two Documents as a
line — a picture of how your notes, people, books and places hang together.

## Getting there

- **Graph** on the Rail, or **Go to Graph** in the palette (Ctrl+K).

## What you see

- **Nodes** — one per Linkable Document, coloured by the Module that owns it.
- **Lines** — a wikilink or a Link field between two Documents, in either direction, drawn once.
- The header counts the nodes and links shown.

## What you can do

- **Find…** — type a name to highlight the matching nodes.
- **Click a node** to open that Document in a [Peek](peek.md).
- **Click an unresolved name** (a "ghost") to create it as a Thought and open it.
- **Filters** (beside Find; on a phone, behind the **Filters** button):
  - **Tags** — only Documents carrying any of the chosen tags.
  - **Unresolved** — show names that are linked but don't exist yet (on by default).
  - **Tags** chip — draw tags as nodes too.
  - **Orphans** — show Documents nothing links to (hidden by default).
  - **One chip per type** — hide or show that type, with its count.
- Your filter choices are remembered on this device.

## The local graph

One Document's neighbourhood — its links, backlinks and unresolved names — shown beside it, and
placeable on a Screen as the **Graph** card (pick the Document, and direct links or two steps).
See [Screens and Cards](screens.md).

## Tips

- Turn on **Orphans** to find notes you never connected to anything.
- Ghost nodes are holes worth filling: names you've linked but never written.
