# Workspaces

A **Workspace** is a separate world of data, with the Modules that world needs: "Personal" for
your life, "Work" for work's Tasks and Research, with no Money in sight.

## What a Workspace decides

- **Its data** — what you see.
  - Your first Workspace is the **default**: it reads everything **shared**.
  - Every Workspace you make after it is **separated**: it sees only what it holds, and nothing
    from the default.
  - Nothing is ever visible from two Workspaces.
- **Its Modules** — which Modules stand on its Rail and offer their capture Commands.
  - Modules never hide data; they only decide what is on the shelf.
  - The Surfaces, the Bases and the command palette are always there, whatever the Modules.
- **Its own arrangements** — its Screens, its Module Overviews, its Phone bar and its capture
  pins.

## What you can do

- **Switch Workspace** — the top of the Rail on the desk, or the name in the phone's header.
  Switching takes you to where you last were in that Workspace.
- **New workspace** — from the same menu, or in [Settings](settings.md). Give it a name and tick
  its Modules.
  - A new Workspace **starts empty**: it sees none of what you already have.
  - Its Phone bar starts from its Modules; rearrange it in Settings.
- **Edit a Workspace** — in [Settings](settings.md) under Workspaces: rename it, change its
  Modules.
- **Move Documents in** — select them in a [Base](bases.md) and choose **Move to workspace**.
  Pick a separated Workspace, or **Shared** for the default. Moved out, a Document stops showing
  where you were.
- **Delete a Workspace** — in Settings, after a confirmation. Nothing is lost:
  - the Documents it held become shared and show in the default Workspace;
  - its Screens move to the default Workspace.
  - The default Workspace, and the last one, cannot be deleted.

## Home Module

Each type of Document has a **Home Module**: where it opens when opened all the way — a Chore in
House, a Category in Money. A type of your own has the Module you choose for it, or none (then it
opens in its Base). See [Documents](documents.md).

## Tips

- A lock beside a Workspace's name means it is separated.
- Want work's Tasks apart from yours? Make a Work Workspace with the Projects Module — no "Work"
  Module needed. Its Tasks are still Tasks.
- Claude reads and writes one Workspace at a time; ask it to `list_workspaces` first. See
  [Claude](connector.md).
