# World

The Module of the people and places in your life. It was called People: the Module keeps its
internal name, so a Workspace or phone bar that already took People shows World with nothing to
redo.

## The map

World opens on one map of every Place and every current Address of the people you know: Places in
Atlas's colour, homes in Contacts'. Click a pin, then **Open**, to go to its Place or Person.

Each Event that is **At** somewhere stands there too, in the Calendar's colour, showing its title
and day: an Event At a Place stands at the Place, and one At a Person stands at their home, the
current Address labelled **Home**, else their first. An Event At a closed or unfound Place, or at a
Person whose chosen Address has no spot, or with no Address, is not on the map. **Open** on an
Event's pin opens the Event in its Peek.

- **Places**, **People** and **Events** chips at the top, each with its count: tap one to hide or
  show its pins. On a phone they sit in the Phone dock.
- **Kind** narrows the Places to one Kind (cafés, museums…), and the Events At them; People's
  pins, and Events At a Person, stay.
- **To try** shows only the Places marked To try, and goes with the Kind: a café near you that you
  haven't tried, and the Events At those Places. People's pins, and Events At a Person, stay.
- Only what has been found stands here: a Place whose address was found and that is not closed, a
  current Address with its spot. Drawing the map looks nothing up. A former Address, or one found nowhere, has no pin.
- With nothing on the map yet, it says how to get something there.

## What is in it

Its places, in order: Map, People, Households, Places.

- **Map** — the home, above.
- **Contacts** — the people you know, or knew, each with a page of their own. It is covered on
  [People](people.md): Everyone, Upcoming, Drifting, Occasions, Ties and Addresses are all unchanged.
- **Households** — the people who live together, listed after People, each with a page of its own.
  It is covered in the Households section of [People](people.md).
- **Atlas** — the places you go (restaurants, cafés, museums, shops), each a **Place** with a page
  of its own. It is covered on [Places](places.md).

## Where it shows

The title World stands in the sidebar, on the phone bar, in a Workspace's settings and on the
Module's own page. A Workspace takes World the way it took People.
