# People

The people in your life, kept by the Contacts App inside [World](world.md): family, friends, colleagues, someone you met once, someone who has
died. Each one is a **Person** you can link to from anywhere, with a page of their own. The People
who live together make a **Household** ("the Smiths"), which you can link to as well.

## People

What you keep here: People, each with a name, the other names they go by (aliases), what they
are to you (their **Relationship**), Tags, and a page of your own words about them.

### The People home

Contacts lands on the People home, which opens on **Upcoming** and then lists **Everyone**: every Person in the current
Workspace, A to Z under a letter each (a name that starts with no letter goes under **#**), with when you last saw them. Click one to open their page.

- **Upcoming** — the Occasions in the next 30 days as a strip of days, soonest first: the day's
  number, how far off, who and which Occasion, and for a Birthday with a known year the age they
  will turn. Each opens the Person. It is a heads-up, never a reminder. It can also be placed as an
  **Upcoming** Card on any Screen, as a list, with its own number of days ahead.
- **Badges** on each row in Everyone: what they are to you, their next Occasion when it is in the
  next 30 days ("Birthday in 9 days · turns 61"), **drifting** with how many days when they are in
  Drifting (below), counted the way Drifting is switched (Not seen or Not mentioned), and their Tags.
- **Search** matches a name or any alias, ignoring case and accents.
- **Tidy given names…** — a notice above Upcoming, shown only while some Person has their given name
  typed as an alias: their name starts with one of their own aliases ("Anne Dupont", alias "Anne") and they
  have no **Given name**. It opens a review, one row per Person, with the proposed **Given name**, **Family
  name** (the rest of the name) and the alias it drops. Untick anyone to leave them as they are;
  **Apply** changes nothing until you press it. Only the duplicated alias goes (their other aliases stay),
  and `[[Anne]]` still reaches them through the Given name. Once no one is left, the notice goes. It never runs by itself.
- **Relationship** (a row of choices, **Everyone** first) and **Tag** narrow the list, and together
  they narrow it further. On a phone they are under **Filters**.
- With no one yet, the home offers **Add person**.

### Drifting

**Drifting** sits above Everyone and answers "who have I lost touch with?". It lists the People
who have a **Relationship** and whom you have **not seen in 90 days** (a fixed window, with no setting): first anyone you have
never seen ("Never seen"), then the longest unseen, each with how many days it has been. Switch
it to **Not mentioned** to count the Days that link to them instead of the Events. Anyone
without a Relationship is not listed. It is only a list: Lifeline never reminds you.

**Leave out** on a row, or **Excluded from Drifting** on a Person's page (under **Edit**) and in their Peek,
takes them out of Drifting, for someone you don't mean to see again. Untick it on their page to
bring them back.

### A Person's page

At the top, their name, then what they are to you (their **Relationship**, underlined with dashes:
click it and type to change it, Enter or leaving it saves, Escape puts it back), then their
**Given name** and **Family name** (both optional, written the same way: "Anne" and "Dupont"), then their age (when their Birthday has a year) and their
aliases, then **Households:** with each Household they belong to as a link, and **Add to a household…**
(see Households, below). Under them, three buttons: **Call**, **Email** and **Profile** reach them by their first
current phone number, email and handle; a former one is skipped. One they have none of is dimmed, and adds one of
its kind: a dimmed **Email** opens the dialog on Email. Their next
Occasion within the year sits beside them.

Below, two columns (one after the other on a phone):

- **The cards** — **Contact details**, **Addresses**, **Occasions** and **Ties**, each a card with
  an **Add** row at its foot. Reading, a number calls, an email mails and a name opens its page.
  **Edit**, at the top of the page, changes them: each row then opens its dialog when clicked,
  a cross removes it (with Undo) and **Former** marks a Contact detail or Address former. Edit also
  lets you rename them in place, as the Relationship is changed (every `[[link]]` to them follows).
  A name can't be emptied ("Name can't be empty.") or be another Person's: the reason shows under
  it, to fix or undo with Escape. Changing the name never changes the Given or Family name, and
  changing those never changes the name: the name stays "Mum" or "Dr Okafor" as you wrote it. Edit also shows **More**: their **Aliases**, their
  **Tags** and **Excluded from Drifting**. **Done** goes back to reading.
- **About them** — the body: what you know of them, written with `[[links]]` and `#tags` like
  any page.
- **Reports** — **Last seen** (the last day of an Event that links to them, through its **With**
  or a `[[link]]` in its notes) and **Last
  mentioned** (the last Day that links to them), side by side and never added together. Seen
  counts an Event once per day it is on, every day of a trip, and nothing in the future; Tasks
  and Occasions never count. **Top seen** (who was on the most days of Events) shows on each
  [Journal](journal.md) period, as **People · Top seen**. A type can also choose **Seen count**, a
  **Seen heatmap** and **Not seen lately** in its editor, and each can be placed as a Report Card.
- **Timeline** — everything that links to them, by month, newest first: Events with them, Tasks
  and Transactions for them, books they recommended, and the Days and notes that name them, each
  once, saying what it is and how it links ("via With", "mentioned", or "via the Smiths" for a link
  to a Household of theirs). A **Today** line splits it:
  above, their Occasions in the year ahead and whatever is dated today or later (a planned Event,
  with how soon it is: "tomorrow", "in 29 days"); below, what is past, then what has no day.
  A note or anything else with no date of its own sits below the line, on the day it last
  changed. An Event still under way (a trip begun but not yet over) stays above the line, by its
  first day, until its End day has passed; one that ends at midnight is over that night. Click one to peek at it. Only an Event counts as having
  seen them: its **With**, or a `[[link]]` to them in its notes ("Dinner with [[Marc]]"). An Event
  **At** their home (its At set to them) is listed, but never counts as having seen them.

Deleting a Person takes them out of every People field that held them (an Event's **With**, a
Task's **For**…); the Events, Tasks and the rest stay. A `[[link]]` to them in a body becomes an
unresolved link, as for any deleted page.

### Contact details

Add one with **Add contact detail** on the Person's page: a **label**, a **kind** (Phone, Email or
Handle) and the number, address or handle as you'd type it. Under **Edit**, click one to change it,
**Former** marks it former, the cross removes it (with Undo).

- **Phone** — a click calls (`tel:`); on a computer, whatever handles calls there answers.
- **Email** — a click opens a new mail to them.
- **Handle** — a click opens the service in a new tab, when Lifeline can tell which: a web
  address you typed (`instagram.com/anne`), a Fediverse address (`@anne@mastodon.social`), or a
  `@name` whose label names the service (Instagram, X or Twitter, GitHub, Telegram, TikTok,
  LinkedIn). Any other handle stays plain text.
- **Former** — a number that stopped working, an account they closed. It moves after the current
  ones, struck through and marked **Former**, with no action, as history; under **Edit**, **Current**
  makes it current again.

Contact details are never on the map, and Claude never sees them (below).
### Occasions

An **Occasion** is a named day in someone's life: a label (**Birthday**, **Met**, **Wedding**, or any
word you choose), a day, and whether it comes back **every year** or happens **once**. Add, edit
and remove them in **Occasions** on the Person's page. Pick the day in the date picker; for a
yearly one whose year you don't know, tick **I don't know the year** and choose a month and a
day instead (29 February included). A once Occasion always needs its year.

- Every Occasion stands on the Calendar's Month, Schedule and Upcoming views and on Today as an
  all-day flag, taking no hours: "🎂 Anne · 65", "⚑ Marc · Surgery". A yearly one comes back every
  year; a once one stands only on its day. It is not an Event, so it can't be dragged and doesn't
  count as seeing someone. It is not a Deadline either: it is never drawn red once its day has
  passed. Click it to peek the Person: their Peek opens with the Occasion's day on top, read-only.
- **Birthday** is the one label Lifeline understands. With the birth year known, the marker carries
  the age they reach ("🎂 Anne · 65") and their page shows how old they are now. With no year
  there is no age: Lifeline never makes up a number.
- A yearly Occasion on **29 February** stands on 29 February in a leap year and on **28 February**
  in the other years, so it is never skipped.
- An Occasion two people share, such as a wedding, is added to each of them.

### Addresses

- **Add address** takes a label and the postal text. Under **Edit**, click one to change it; the cross removes it, with Undo. When you save it, Lifeline looks the place
  up (OpenStreetMap's Nominatim) and puts a **Pin** on the Map, labelled "Marc · Work". A
  Person with two current Addresses stands on the Map twice.
- When someone moves, mark it **Former** under **Edit**: the Address leaves the Map and stays on their page, below
  the current ones, as history. **Current** brings it back.
- If the lookup finds nothing, or fails, the Address is still saved and shown, marked "not on
  the map", with **Try again**. The message says which: no place found means check the text; the
  lookup could not be reached means try again later. Changing an Address's text looks it up afresh.
- A shared home is held by each Person who lives there: add it to each.
- Claude never sees a Person's Addresses or their Pins (see below).

### Ties

A **Tie** says how two People stand to each other. Press **Add tie** on either Person's page,
find the other Person by name, and give a word for each end ("mother" and "daughter"). It is
stored once and shows on both pages, each read from its own side, with the other Person as a
link. The dialog asks both ways round, by name: "Léa is Anne's…" and "Anne is Léa's…". The words
are yours: "godmother", "ex" and "best friend's brother" all fit.

Once you have picked the other Person, a row of **preset chips** sits under the first question:
daughter, son, child, sister, brother, sibling, mother, father, parent, wife, husband, partner,
the grandparent and grandchild words, niece, nephew, aunt, uncle and cousin. Tap one and both
words fill in: tap "daughter" on Anne's page and Anne's word becomes "mother" (or "father", or
"parent"). Lifeline picks it from what you have already written about Anne: if every preset word
in her own Ties is female, "mother"; if all are male, "father"; with mixed words, or none yet,
"parent". Nobody is asked their gender. The chip you tapped shows as pressed.

A chip also sets the Tie's **kind**, what the words mean (parent and child, sibling, partner and
so on). The words stay yours to change: "mum" in place of "mother" keeps the kind. Tap the
pressed chip again to clear the kind and keep the words. A Tie with only free words has no kind
and works as it always did. A Tie you made before chips existed whose words are a matching pair
("mother" and "daughter", in any case) counts as that kind, with nothing rewritten. Claude reads
Ties as words only. Change or remove it under **Edit**
on either page (with Undo) and both change. A Tie is never to you, only between two People.
Deleting a Person removes their Ties; the other Person's page is untouched. A Tie is not a link
in a body, so it adds no Backlink and counts for no Report.

**Suggested ties.** From Ties that have a kind, each Person's Ties card **suggests** the Ties that follow, under a
**Suggested** heading: with Paul the father of Tom and of Lily, Lily's card offers "Tom is Lily's
brother" and Tom's "Lily is Tom's sister" (the words follow what you wrote: Tom is a "son", Lily a
"daughter"). Lifeline suggests, one step at a time:

- children of the same parent as siblings;
- a parent's parent as a grandparent;
- a parent's brother or sister as an aunt or uncle, and their children as cousins;
- a brother's or sister's parent as a parent;
- a parent's partner as a parent ("Alice is Lily's mother", with Alice Paul's wife).

Nothing is added on its own. **Add** makes it a real Tie, shown on both pages like any other, and
any Ties that follow from it are suggested in turn. **Dismiss** hides it on both pages for good,
whatever Ties change later; **Undo** on the message brings it back, and there is no other list of
dismissed ones. With two or more suggestions, **Add all** adds them at once. Two People already
tied in any way, even by free words, are never suggested, and neither is anyone outside this
Workspace. Free-word Ties suggest nothing, and nor does Claude: suggestions live on the card only.

Removing anything from a Person's page (an Occasion, an Address, a Contact detail or a Tie)
happens at once, with **Undo** on the message that follows: it puts back just that one, even
if you have added something since.

### Households

A **Household** is the People who live together: Paul, Mary, Tom and Lily, "the Smiths". It has a
name, aliases, **members** you choose, and a page of your own words. It is not a family: Paul's
mother in another town is his Tie, not a member, and Mary keeps her own name and is still one.

- **Households**, beside **People** in the People Module (its sidebar, or its tabs at the bottom,
  against the bar, on a phone), lists every Household in the current Workspace,
  A to Z, with the names and number of its current members (former members only on its page).
  **Add household** takes a name and opens its page.
- **A Household's page** — its name, underlined with dashes (click it to rename it; every
  `[[link]]` to it follows), its **Members**, its **Aliases** and **About it**, its body, then its
  own **Reports** (**Last seen** and **Last mentioned**) and **Timeline**: what links to the
  Household itself, by name, each opening in a Peek. **Add member** finds a Person by name or
  alias; the cross beside a member takes them out, with Undo.
  Each member's name opens their page. The calendar button beside a member sets **Since** and
  **Until**, the first and last day they lived there; either may stay empty. Someone who moves out
  gets an Until day and, once it has passed, moves to **Former members**, listed apart with
  their days, never removed.
- **From a Person's page**, **Add to a household…** finds a Household by name, or makes a new one
  from the name you type ("New household “Student flat”") with them in it.
- **Adding a Person** whose Family name matches a member of a Household suggests joining it
  (see **Add person…**, below); nothing joins unless you tap the suggestion.
- Someone may belong to several Households: a student's flat and their parents' home.
- Only People of the Household's own Workspace can be its members. A Person moved to another
  Workspace leaves the Households of the old one; a Household moved keeps only the members that
  are in its new Workspace (move them together to keep them).
- In an Event's **With**, typing a Household's name offers it ("Household · Paul Smith, Mary
  Martin…"); picking it adds its members of today as People, without doubling anyone already
  there. Take off whoever was away: **With** records who was there, and keeps the People, never
  the Household.
- `[[the Smiths]]` links to the Household like any page, and clicking it opens its **Peek**,
  where you can change its aliases, members and Tags; **Open** goes to its page.
- **A link to a Household counts for its members.** "Dinner at `[[the Smiths]]`" in a Day or an
  Event stands on each member's **Timeline** ("via the Smiths") and counts toward their **Last
  seen** and **Last mentioned**, so toward **Drifting** too, as if you had named each of them. A
  Day or Event that names someone and their Household is one entry, as the direct link.
- **A link counts for whoever lived there on its date.** The Day's date, or an Event's days, decide
  which members it reaches (Since and Until included), so a past dinner keeps counting for someone
  who has since left, and does not count for someone who arrived later. A page with no date of its
  own, a note, reaches only members with neither Since nor Until.
- Deleting a Person takes them out of every Household; the Households stay. Deleting a
  Household changes no Person.
- The home stays each member's **Address**: a Household has none, and no Occasion or Contact
  detail.

### Linking to a Person

- Write `[[` and their name, or any of their aliases: `[[Mum]]` and `[[Anne Dupont]]` land on
  the same Person. The completer marks each one as a Person, with their Relationship.
- Their **Given name** works like an alias you never type: with "Anne" as Anne Dupont's Given
  name, `[[Anne]]` lands on her, the completer offers "Anne → Anne Dupont", and a plain "Anne"
  in a Day shows under her **Unlinked mentions**. It is read from the Given name, never copied
  into her Aliases, so correcting the Given name changes what `[[Anne]]` reaches (a page titled
  "Anne" still comes first, as it does over an alias). Two People with the same Given name share
  it as two equal aliases would: `[[Anne]]` goes to one of them, so write the full name to be sure.
- Click a link to a Person to open them in a **Peek**, where you can change their Given name, Family name, aliases,
  Relationship and Tags and write in their body. **Open** goes to their page.
- Select a name in a Day (or any body) and pick **Person** under **People** in the Selection
  menu: the Person is made, and the name becomes a link to them.

### Cards

- **People by Relationship** — your People grouped under each Relationship, with how many there
  are under it; each name opens their page. People with no Relationship are left out. Where: a
  Card you can place on any Screen.

### Commands

- **Add person…** — a new Person, from a name, or from a **Given name** and a **Family name**: until
  you type a name, it fills in as "Anne Dupont" while you type the two, and a name you typed
  ("Mum") is kept as typed. Both names are kept on the Person, beside the name, and can be left
  empty. When the **Family name** is one a member of a Household of this Workspace has ("Smith",
  and the Smiths have a Smith in them), the dialog offers a **Join the Smiths** button: tap it (it stays pressed) and the new
  Person joins when you press **Add**; leave it, and they join nothing. It is only ever a
  suggestion, and an unrelated Smith simply isn't tapped. Where: the Capture sheet, the palette
  (Ctrl+K).
- **Add occasion…** — a birthday, anniversary or other Occasion for a Person found by name,
  alias or id; it has no screen, and is how Claude adds Occasions (`people_occasion_add`). Where: Claude
  only.
- **Go to People** — the People home. Where: the palette.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers **Add person…** under People: just a name (or the two names), so
you can add someone you just met before you forget it.

## Ask Claude

- "Add my aunt Anne Dupont, I call her Mum" — `people_add`, with a name, optional Given and
  Family names, aliases, a Relationship and Tags.
- "Anne's birthday is 12 November 1961" — `people_occasion_add`, for the Person found by name,
  alias, Given name or id (accents and case aside), with a label, a day (the year optional) and yearly or
  once. An unknown name is refused; so is one that fits two People — a name they share, or one's
  name and the other's alias — and the refusal lists each with their id, so Claude can ask which
  and call again with it.
- "Whose birthday is coming up?" — `people_upcoming` is the Upcoming Report for the next days,
  with the age each Birthday reaches; `agenda` lists Occasions too, as `occasion` entries apart
  from Deadlines, with the same age.
- "When did I last see Anne?" — `get_document` on a Person adds their Last seen and Last
  mentioned, from what Claude is allowed to read.
- "Who is Léa to Anne?" — `get_document` on a Person also lists their Ties, read from their side
  ("daughter: Léa"), leaving out anyone in the Private folder.
- "What have I written about Anne?" — `search_documents`, `get_document`.
- "Who lives at the Smiths'?" — `get_document` on a Household gives its name, aliases and its
  members by name, with the days each lived there when set (a past Until marks a former member), leaving
  out anyone in the Private folder; nothing of a member's Addresses or
  Contact details.

### What Claude sees of a Person

Claude reads a Person's name, Given and Family name, aliases, Relationship, Tags, Occasions, Ties, body, and any Field you
added to Person yourself, and it can change those; it can add an Occasion.

Claude never sees a Person's **Addresses** or **Contact details**, current or former, from any
tool: searching for words of an address or a phone number finds nobody, and a Person's Pins on the
map are not offered to it either. Claude can't add, change or mark former an Address or a Contact
detail: you type those in Lifeline yourself, so a hidden instruction can never swap a friend's
number for someone else's.

Two things the wall doesn't cover:

- **The body is yours.** A street or a number you write in a Person's body is read like any
  body. Lifeline doesn't look for addresses in what you write; keep them in the Person's Addresses
  and Contact details instead.
- **The Private folder** still hides a whole Person from Claude, name and all.

## Tips

- A Relationship is your own words ("my aunt", "climbing partner"), not a fixed list. Tags are
  for grouping People your own way.
- Someone in both your work and your life is two People, one in each Workspace, so each
  Workspace stays whole.
- An Author in the Library or an Artist in the Museum you have never met stays there; People is
  for people in your life. When you come to know one, choose **Turn into a Person…** on the
  Author's page (the **…** menu) or on the Artist's page in the Vault, and confirm. The same
  Document becomes a Person: its links, aliases and notes carry over, it joins People, and it
  stays an Author or Artist, so the Library and the Museum still show it and its works still
  name it.
- A type of your own you already made for people keeps its Documents as they are.
