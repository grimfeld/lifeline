# Thoughts

What comes straight out of your head — an idea, a feeling, a reflection, a question — with no
Source and no date to tie it to. A stream, not an inbox: nothing here asks you to process it.

## Thoughts

What you keep here: Thoughts, each with an optional kind (Idea, Feeling, Reflection, Question).

### The stream

- One box at the top: write, pick a kind if you like, and **Keep** (or Ctrl+Enter, ⌘+Enter).
  The first line becomes the title.
- Below, every Thought, newest first, grouped by day.
- Narrow to one kind. On a phone the kind sits in the Phone dock.
- Open a Thought to write more in it, link it, tag it or delete it.

### Thoughts and Notes

- A Thought never has a Source. Give it one from its side panel and it becomes a Note on that
  Source in [Notes](notes.md); take the Source away and it is a Thought again. Links to it
  survive either way.

### Commands

- **New thought…** — one Thought, with an optional kind. Where: the Capture sheet, the palette
  (Ctrl+K).
- **Go to Thoughts** — the stream. Where: the palette.

### Cards

- **Recent thoughts** — the newest Thoughts, one line each. Settings: how many, and one kind or
  every kind.

## Capture

**+** on the phone (or Ctrl+K at the desk) offers **New thought…** under Thoughts.

## Ask Claude

- "Note this thought: maybe the garden needs a pond" — `thoughts_capture`, with an optional kind
  (idea, feeling, reflection, question), title and tags.
- "What was I thinking about last week?" — `search_documents`, `get_document`.

## Tips

- Don't sort or empty the stream: Thoughts are kept, not cleared. Find them again by search,
  Tag or link.
- A feeling about the whole day belongs in its Mood, in [Progress](progress.md); a Thought is prose.
