# Research

Writing that argues from your material: Essays built on your Notes, Quotes, Sources and
Thoughts — an essay, an article, a thesis, a talk.

## Research

What you keep here: Essays, each with a status (Draft, Revised, Done), and the Versions you save
of them.

### Essays — the desk

- Every Essay, grouped by topic. A topic is a Tag: a thesis's chapters are the Essays sharing
  one, and an Essay with two Tags sits under both. Essays with no Tag are under **No topic**.
- Each shows its status, opening words, word count, how much material is pinned, and when it
  was last edited.
- Narrow by status. On a phone the status and **New essay** sit in the Phone dock.
- **New essay** — a title and topic Tags; it opens straight away.

### Writing an Essay

- The Essay in the middle; the **material** beside it (on a phone, behind **Material**).
- **Material** — what the Essay links to with `[[ ]]`, plus what you pinned. Unpin with the pin
  icon.
- **Candidates** — pages one link away from your material, offered but never added on their
  own. **Pin as material** to add one.
- A small graph of the Essay's neighbourhood.
- Open a piece of material and it opens as a tab beside the Essay, to write in right there.
  Tabs are remembered per Essay on this device.

### Versions

- Save the current text on purpose, with an optional label: the camera in the side panel. Open
  the panel with its button in the page's header.
- Pick a version to see it beside the current text, and **Restore** it — a new edit, so nothing
  is lost.
- The panel also counts the words.
- Chapters in [Manuscript](manuscript.md) keep Versions the same way.

### Reading aloud

- In reading mode an Essay has a stopwatch under it — start, pause, reset — for giving it as a
  talk. Mark a talk with a `talk` Tag.

### Commands

- **Go to Research** — the desk of Essays. Where: the palette (Ctrl+K).

### Cards

- **Drafts** — Essays not yet done, most recently edited first: pick up where you left off.
  Can be narrowed by Tags.

## Capture

Research has nothing on the Capture sheet: an Essay is started at the desk.

## Ask Claude

Essays are Documents, so Claude works with them through the general tools.

- "What have I drafted on Stoicism?" — `search_documents`, `get_document`.
- "Start an essay called *On Patience*, tagged `stoicism`" — `create_document`.
- "Retitle it" or "set it to Revised" — `update_document`. A rename rewrites the links to it.

## Tips

- Link material from the text as you write (`[[`), and the material list fills itself.
- Use the same Tag on every chapter of a long piece to keep them together on the desk.
