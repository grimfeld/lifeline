# The Hub and its Windows

On a desk or a large tablet, the **Hub** is how you go between places and arrange them: an index
of what you can open, and a stage where each one opens in its own **Window**. Open the Money
Module beside the Calendar and see both at once.

(On a phone there are no Windows: see [Using Lifeline on a phone](phone.md).)

## The Hub

- **The top bar** — LIFELINE, the Workspace picker, **Bases**, the sync status, and the date and
  time.
- **The index** (left) — this Workspace's Modules in their order, then Calendar, Map, Graph and
  Screens. **Guide** and **Settings** sit at its foot.
- **The stage** — the rest of the screen, where your Windows are.
- A dot beside a row means its Window is open. The row of the Window in front is in the accent
  colour.

## Windows

- **Open** — pick a row in the index. The Window takes 60% of the stage, cascaded from the last
  one, or sits where you last left it.
- **Focus** — pick a row that is already open, or click anywhere in a Window, to bring it to the
  front.
- **Move** — drag its bar. At least 80px of the bar always stays on the stage.
- **Resize** — drag its bottom-right corner. A Window is never smaller than 320 × 240.
- **Close** — the button on its bar.
- Each Window keeps its own place in its Module: the Money Window stays on its Budget while you
  use the Calendar's.
- There is one Window for each Module or Surface. A link to a Module's page (from a Peek, a
  link, the palette) opens that Module's Window, or brings it to the front.
- The address of the page is the front Window's.
- Bases, Settings, the Guide and the Vault open in a Window too, but they are pages, not places kept on the desk: their Window closes when another Window comes to the front.
- Back and Forward move within the Window in front: each Window keeps its own history. Coming to the front or closing a Window adds no step of its own.

## Narrow Windows

A Window lays its content out for its own width, not the screen's. Make one narrow and the
Module shows its narrow layout, but it never grows the phone's Phone bar.

## Remembered

- The Windows you have open, where they are, their size and which is in front are kept for this
  device and this Workspace, and come back when you reload.
- Switching Workspace swaps to that Workspace's Windows.
- A new desk opens where you last were, as one Window filling the stage.
- Close the last Window and the stage says "Nothing is open. Pick a Module on the left: each
  opens in its own Window."

## Tips

- Open the Calendar and a Module side by side to plan while you work.
- **Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac) goes to any place from the keyboard; see [Capture](capture.md).
