# Map

The **Map** shows every Document that has a place, of every type, as a pin on one OpenStreetMap:
museums visited, people's homes, places from your Journal.

## Getting there

- **Map** on the Rail, or **Go to Map** in the palette (Ctrl+K).
- Put it in a Phone bar slot to have it one tap away.

## What you can do

- **Pan and zoom** like any map.
- **Tap a pin** to see its name (and a line about it), then **Open →** to open the Document.
- **Type chips** at the top — one per type that has places, with its count. Tap one to hide or
  show that type. On a phone the chips sit in the Phone dock. The choice is forgotten when you
  leave.
- Pins are coloured by the Module that owns them.

## Getting things on the map

- A type shows up here once it has a **Coordinates** field. Add one to any type in
  [Bases](bases.md) (edit the type › add a Field › Coordinates).
- Fill in the coordinates on a Document and it gets a pin. A Document with an address but no
  coordinates has no pin yet.
- No such type yet? The Map says so: "No Document type is Locatable yet".

## Elsewhere

- **Map** card — the same map on a Screen or an Overview, all types or one. See
  [Screens and Cards](screens.md).
- **Map** layout — one Base's Documents on a map, by a coordinates field you pick. It lists the
  ones it could not place. See [Bases](bases.md).

## Tips

- Use the chips to narrow down when the map gets crowded.
