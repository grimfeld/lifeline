# The Swear Jar is its own App, not a price on Negative activities

The user wanted to fine themself money for things they do — trust-based, no accountability, the jar starting over each month. Life Level already has a list of bad behaviour, the **Negative activity**, and the obvious move was to give it an optional price so one log both drains XP and drops a coin. That was rejected: a Negative is meaningless without a Habit to hit (logging with none is blocked, by design), so every jar item would need a rival Habit or a random roll it has no use for, and deleting a Habit deletes the Negative Completions that hit it ("honest quitting") — which would silently shrink months of the jar. Putting the jar in Pocket as a Spend against a "Jar" Allowance was rejected too: an Allowance is money the user *has*, the jar is money the user *owes*. So the **Swear Jar** is a separate App on the Progress Module with its own list — **Offences** (named, priced, a face) and **Fines** (one incident each) — and the two lists of bad behaviour are deliberate: one costs XP, the other costs money, and one thing may sit on both.

## Consequences

- A Fine carries its Offence's price as it was when logged — the opposite of Life Level, where Difficulty and Gravity re-value the whole ledger. A closed month never moves.
- The month is a window, not an event: the jar is the calendar month's Fines, derived at read. Nothing is reset, emptied or paid out, and there is no settling mark.
- An Offence with Fines is archived, never deleted, so past months stay whole.
- An Offence is not Loggable: no other App fines anyone. A bridge from Negative activities (or Chores) may come later through that Contract, as an explicit choice.
