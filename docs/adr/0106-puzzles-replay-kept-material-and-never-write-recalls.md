# Puzzles replay kept material and never write Recalls

The user spends daily time on word games (Wordle, the mini crossword, Connections) and wants that habit to serve what Knowledge keeps. Knowledge is retention, never acquisition (ADR 0073), and a Recall is the user's own pass or fail, never typed in or checked by the App (ADR 0058). A word game is the opposite on both counts: it checks typed answers, and the commercial ones supply the words.

- A **Puzzle** is a Knowledge Document: one word game, in one format, over one Topic. It is built only from Words, Sentences and Facts that are already kept, not paused, and already met in a Practice. It never uses a new Prompt and never uses material Lifeline supplies, not even a dictionary to validate guesses (any string of the right length is accepted).
- Playing writes **no Recall** and moves no schedule. A Puzzle is extra exposure and play. A Practice stays the only place Recalls are written.
- "Already met" means the Prompt a Puzzle uses has at least one Recall, pass or fail: a Word's reverse Prompt, or the only Prompt of a Fact or a blank. In Connections, any Prompt of the item counts.
- A day starts at local midnight, the same boundary as a Topic's Tally. An unfinished daily Puzzle stays unsolved after midnight and never tallies.
- A Puzzle is kept as a fact: its format, its Topic, daily or replay, what it drew (fixed when first opened), each guess, and solved or unsolved. One daily Puzzle per format per Topic, drawn toward what is due soon, plus unlimited replays.
- Knowledge still shows no score or streak. A Topic gains a second Tally occurrence, each daily Puzzle finished (solved or not; replays never). Life Level is where a Puzzle's reward lives.
- Formats, in delivery order: Wordle-like (clue is a Word's meaning, guess its one-word term, 6 guesses); a free-form crossword (5–8 crossing answers from Word terms, single-word Fact answers and Sentence blanks); Connections (4 of the Topic's Decks, 4 items each, 4 mistakes). No Spelling Bee: it asks for words never kept.

## Considered options

- **A solved Puzzle writes pass Recalls, a failed one fail.** Rejected. Letter feedback and crossing letters are hints, so passes would inflate and the FSRS schedule would drift from what is really held. It would also break "the grade is always the user's".
- **The user grades each item at the end of a Puzzle.** Rejected. That is a Practice with extra steps, and it makes play into homework.
- **Puzzles that bring new words (bundled word lists, dictionaries).** Rejected. That is acquisition, which ADR 0073 keeps out. Real NYT games stay outside, and what is met there arrives through an Intake.
- **Score and streaks inside Knowledge.** Rejected. Retention is the one figure Knowledge gives. Life Level already owns XP and streaks, reached through a Tally.
- **Puzzles over "everything".** Rejected. A crossword mixing Spanish and History reads badly, and the Tally belongs to a Topic.

## Consequences

- Puzzles cannot help or hurt a schedule, so they can be added, changed or dropped without re-valuing any Prompt.
- A small vocabulary limits what can be built. A Topic without enough eligible material simply shows no Puzzle of that format, so a dense NYT-style 5×5 grid is out and the crossword is free-form.
- Without a dictionary, a guess can be nonsense. That is accepted: the cost is one wasted guess, and the alternative is outside material.
- Matching folds case and accents, and the correct spelling is shown on the grid and in the reveal. Whether ñ folds to n is a per-Topic setting, folding by default.
- Deleting a Word leaves the Puzzles already played: they are facts and show it as deleted. A Tally that fired stays fired, as every Tally does.
- No MCP tool plays or starts a Puzzle. The Guide page for Knowledge covers them.
