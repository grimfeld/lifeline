Lifeline Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Lifeline is a personal organiser. This page explains what data it handles and where that data lives.

Your Lifeline account

The documents you create in Lifeline (tasks, notes, events and the like) are stored in your browser and synced to Lifeline's server so they reach your other devices. They are visible only to your account. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Gmail

Lifeline no longer connects to Gmail or any other mailbox, and asks Google for no permission. If you connected a Gmail account to an earlier version, Lifeline holds no access to it; you can remove the old entry on your Google Account's third-party access page.
