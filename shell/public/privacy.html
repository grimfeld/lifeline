Lifeline Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Lifeline is a personal organiser. This page explains what data it handles, where that data lives, and what happens when you connect a Gmail account to its Mail screen.

Your Lifeline account

The documents you create in Lifeline (tasks, notes, events and the like) are stored in your browser and synced to Lifeline's server so they reach your other devices. They are visible only to your account. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Gmail data

When you connect a Gmail account, Lifeline asks Google for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify permission. It uses it to:

  • list and read your threads, messages and labels so you can see them in Lifeline;
  • apply the actions you choose — archive, mark read or unread, star, label, trash.

Lifeline does not send email. Reply and compose open Gmail itself.

How Gmail data is handled:

  • Google's access token is kept in your browser's memory only, for about an hour. No refresh token is ever requested or stored.
  • Messages are read directly from Google's API by your browser. A cache of the threads and messages you have opened is kept in that browser's local storage so Mail can show them offline. That cache is never sent to Lifeline's server.
  • Gmail data reaches Lifeline's server only when you explicitly keep a message — for example, turning it into a task or a clipping. That copy is then an ordinary Lifeline document in your account, and you can delete it.
  • Gmail data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Lifeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Deleting a Mailbox in Lifeline removes its cached messages from that device. To revoke Lifeline's access to your Gmail account entirely, go to your Google Account's third-party access page.
